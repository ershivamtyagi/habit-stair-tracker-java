Common issues

  • If the app does not open, reinstall using the latest installer.
  • If your recent activity looks empty, check whether local app data was removed on the device.
  • If you want to reset today’s entry, click today’s stair again or remove the entry from the recent days list.

App behavior

  • The app stores data locally on the device.
  • The app does not require an account.
  • One entry is tracked per calendar day.
