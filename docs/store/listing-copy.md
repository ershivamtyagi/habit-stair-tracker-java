## App name

Stair Progress App

## Short description

Track one completed activity per day and watch your weekly progress climb up a visual 8-step stair.

## Full description

Stair Progress App is a simple desktop habit tracker built for people who want a lightweight daily check-in without the clutter of a full productivity suite.

Each calendar day is represented as one step on an 8-day progress stair. Add a short note for today, mark the step complete, and watch the climber move upward as your streak grows.

The app is designed for quick personal use:

- Save one activity note per day
- See recent days in a clean timeline
- Track your current streak
- Click today's stair to mark it complete or clear it
- Keep your data stored locally on your device

Stair Progress App works well for routines such as exercise, study, reading, journaling, or any small daily commitment you want to keep visible.

## Features

- One-click daily completion
- 8-day visual stair progress view
- Simple recent activity timeline
- Local-only storage on your Windows device
- Fast startup with no account required
