Daily Activity Climb

One stair per day.

Stair Progress App is a lightweight Windows habit tracker for daily completion notes, simple streak tracking, and a visual 8-step progress stair.

Privacy Policy Support

What It Does

Track one activity note per day, review recent entries, and keep a visible streak without creating an account.

Data Storage

The app stores your entries locally on your Windows device under your user profile.
