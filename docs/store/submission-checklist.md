# Microsoft Store Submission Checklist

## Ready in repo

- Windows installer: `dist\Stair Progress App-1.0.0.msi`
- Store listing draft: `docs\store\listing-copy.md`
- Privacy policy draft: `docs\store\privacy-policy.html`
- Support page draft: `docs\store\support.html`

## Still required before submission

- Replace placeholder support email with a real email address
- Publish the privacy policy page at a public HTTPS URL
- Publish the support page at a public URL or provide a support email in Partner Center
- Capture at least 1 desktop screenshot from the running app
- Upload a 1:1 Store logo / box art image
- Reserve the app name in Partner Center
- Complete pricing, availability, and age ratings

## Recommended

- Upload 4 to 6 screenshots instead of only 1
- Use a short feature list in the description
- Verify install, launch, update, and uninstall on a clean Windows machine
- Consider code signing for distribution outside the Store
