Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Stair Progress App.

Summary

Stair Progress App is a local desktop app for tracking one activity note per day. The app is designed to work without creating an account and without sending your activity data to a remote server.

What data the app stores

The app stores the following information on your device:

  • The calendar date for each saved entry
  • The activity note you enter
  • The local date and time when the entry was saved

Where the data is stored

On Windows, the app stores its data locally in your user profile under the Local App Data folder. The data is not intended to be uploaded by the app to the developer.

What data the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in information
  • No payment information
  • No location information
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • No analytics or advertising identifiers built into this version

How the data is used

Your data is used only to provide the app's core functionality, including showing your recent entries, your current streak, and the stair progress view.

Sharing

The app does not intentionally share your activity data with the developer or third parties.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete entries in the app or remove the app and its local data.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.
